About Elite

Security training designed for real operational work

Elite Protection Training prepares learners for security roles through structured tuition, practical scenarios and clear guidance on professional standards. Training is focused on the knowledge, confidence and conduct required in public-facing security environments.

Our programmes support new entrants, licensed operatives seeking further development and experienced professionals progressing towards supervisory or instructional roles.
