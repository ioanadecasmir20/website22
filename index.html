About Elite Protection Training

Elite Protection Training focuses on turning security qualifications into real-world capability. Our trainers come from operational security, door supervision, CCTV and management roles and bring live experience into every session.

We blend classroom delivery, scenario-based exercises and eLearning so that learners understand not only what the standards require, but how to apply them on shift at 3am when it really matters.
